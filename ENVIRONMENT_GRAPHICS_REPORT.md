# ECLIPSE: THE LAST CIVILIZATION
## ENVIRONMENT GRAPHICS REPORT (PHASE 3 — GREYFALL CITY & WORLD)

### 1. Greyfall City Boulevard Transformation
The urban environment was overhauled from generic primitive boxes into an authentic post-apocalyptic evacuation corridor.

#### Environmental Features & Believability:
- **Asphalt Road Surface:** Mapped with procedural cracked tarmac texture, aggregate stone flecks, weathered potholes, and oil spill puddles.
- **Centerline Road Markings:** Double solid yellow lines with realistic weathering gaps and erosion segments.
- **Dashed Lane Markings & Crosswalks:** White dashed highway dividers and pedestrian zebra crossings with tire skid marks.
- **Abandoned Vehicle Fleet:**
  * Abandoned Police Cruiser with open driver door, light bar, and rotating red siren illumination.
  * Civilian Evacuation Sedan with strapped rooftop luggage carrier and suitcases.
  * Abandoned Military Transport Truck with heavy tire treads and canvas canopy.
- **Defensive Fortifications & Barricades:**
  * Concrete Jersey barriers with warning hazard stencils.
  * Timber log palisades with barbed wire fencing.
  * Fortified sandbag revetments protecting street intersections.
  * Military watchtower with perimeter spotlight and survivor banner.
- **Atmospheric Street Props:**
  * Weathered metal streetlights casting warm downward light cones.
  * Explodable red fuel barrels with caution hazard stencils.
  * Military olive ammunition crates with brass latch closures.
  * Brass survivor lanterns with warm flame glow.

### 2. Multi-Sector Environment Architectural Roadmap
1. **Sector 01: Greyfall City** — Urban evacuation boulevard, abandoned vehicles, concrete barricades, commercial storefronts with boarded windows.
2. **Sector 02: Blackwood Forest** — Dense pine wilderness, muddy tire tracks, fallen logs, and low-lying ground mist.
3. **Sector 03: Ironshield Military Base** — Reinforced blast doors, shipping containers, armored vehicle wrecks, and razor wire perimeters.
4. **Sector 04: Ashen Industrial District** — Rusted pipe gantries, smokestacks, slag heaps, and industrial boilers.
5. **Sector 05: ECLIPSE Research Complex** — High-tech bio-containment laboratories, shattered cleanrooms, and flickering emergency lights.
