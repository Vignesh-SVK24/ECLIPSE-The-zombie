# ECLIPSE: THE LAST CIVILIZATION
## UI VISUAL REDESIGN REPORT (PHASE 5 — LEFT TO SURVIVE TACTICAL INTERFACE)

### 1. Visual Language & Composition
The UI was overhauled to deliver a dark, tactical, military survival aesthetic inspired by *Left to Survive*:
- **Color Palette:** Dark charcoal backgrounds (`#111918`), muted military olive (`#3d4238`), weathered amber accents (`#C49B58`), and warning crimson (`#ff4444`).
- **Typography:** Heavy high-contrast tactical headers, tracked letter-spacing (2px–4px), and clear hierarchical labels.
- **Glassmorphism & Texture:** 12px blurred backdrop filters, subtle metallic borders, and semi-transparent dark panels that allow the 3D cinematic world to show through.

---

### 2. UI Subsystems & Features
1. **3D Main Menu & Hero Showcase:**
   - 360-degree drag rotation allowing the player to inspect Ethan Cross's full 3D tactical rig, backpack, and weapons in real-time.
   - Clean tactical category navigators (`NEW GAME`, `MISSIONS`, `WEAPONS`, `INVENTORY`, `SETTLEMENT`, `SETTINGS`).
2. **Tactical In-Game HUD:**
   - Left top status panel: Health & Stamina bars with segmented military track styling.
   - Right top tactical panel: Evacuation sector objective tag, active threat indicator, and network simulation status badge.
   - Bottom right weapon card: Active firearm name, caliber designation, and ammo clip/reserve counter.
   - Precision military crosshair with hit confirmation bloom.
3. **Responsive Mobile Action Buttons:**
   - Ergonomic 6-button touch cluster on lower-right (`CAM`, `GUN`, `RELOAD`, `RUN`, `AIM`, `FIRE`).
   - Dynamic floating virtual joystick with auto-sprint forward threshold indicator.
   - Full safe-area inset adaptation for iPhone Dynamic Island and Android punch-hole cameras.
