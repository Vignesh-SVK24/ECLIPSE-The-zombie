# MAP ENVIRONMENT EXPANSION REPORT — ECLIPSE: THE LAST CIVILIZATION

## 1. Executive Summary
- **Target Map**: Greyfall City Evacuation Sector 1 & Checkpoint Boulevard.
- **Visual Style**: Gritty, semi-realistic post-apocalyptic zombie survival aesthetic inspired by *Left to Survive*.
- **Map Dimensions**: 32m wide multi-lane asphalt boulevard spanning 240m along Z-axis (-120m to +120m).
- **Total Registered Obstacles**: 26 active physical obstacles with bounding boxes and collision meshes.
- **New Zones**:
  1. *Zone A: Evacuation Ambulatory & Medical Quarantine* (Z: -60 to -40)
  2. *Zone B: Crashed Civilian & Police Chokepoint* (Z: -35 to 0)
  3. *Zone C: Industrial Alley & Storefront Collision* (Z: 0 to +20)
  4. *Zone D: Military Fortification Checkpoint & Interactive Gate* (Z: +20 to +40)
  5. *Zone E: Heavy Industrial Depots & Shipping Yard* (Z: +40 to +110)

## 2. Environmental Storytelling Additions
- **Damaged & Abandoned Vehicles**: Crashed Police Cruiser with active red siren light, Civilian Evacuation Sedan with roof luggage, Heavy Military Cargo Transport Truck, Paramedic Emergency Ambulance with rear open doors, Burnt Charred SUV with smoking engine and flame, Commercial Delivery Box Van crashed into storefront, Overturned Pickup Truck with oil slick, and Bullet-riddled Station Wagon.
- **Road Destruction & Decals**: Asphalt cracks, potholes with reflective stagnant water, blood pools, dragging blood smear trails, infected bloody footprints, glinting windshield safety glass shards, and spent brass bullet casings around sandbag bunkers.
- **Infrastructure**: Fallen utility pole with sparking copper wires, damaged municipal bus stop shelter, weathered streetlights (working and flickering sodium lamps), heavy steel dumpsters, and industrial shipping container.
